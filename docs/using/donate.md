# Donate

The **Donate** tab has the two monthly tiers, a one-time donation and the supporters wall. Payments open in Stripe in your browser. Sign in with Discord in the launcher to subscribe.

## Tiers

- **Supporter**, $5 a month: no ads in the launcher, access to #f1-supporter-leaks, the #f1-supporter-general chat, and Discord embed links and external emoji permissions.
- **Supporter+**, $20 a month: all of Supporter, early access to addons in testing and access to private betatesting.

Select **Subscribe** under a tier to pay for it in Stripe Checkout. Your role and perks show up in the launcher and on Discord within a minute.

## One-time donation

Enter an amount in dollars and select **Donate**. A donation from a signed-in account converts to whole months of a tier:

- $20 or more: one month of Supporter+ for each $20.
- $5 to $19.99: one month of Supporter for each $5.
- Less than $5: no role.

**Claim previous donation** links a payment made outside the launcher to your Discord account. Enter the email you paid with on Stripe and the invoice or payment ID from the receipt, then select **Claim**.

## Your listing

While you're a supporter, the tab shows your tier and the months left. **Edit your listing** sets the line shown next to your name on the supporters wall, and whether you show as your Discord name and avatar or as **Anonymous**.

## Manage your subscription

With a subscription, **Manage subscription** shows under **Edit your listing**, and the tier buttons change:

- **Manage subscription**, and **Manage** under your tier, open Stripe's customer portal in your browser.
- **Switch** under the other tier opens the portal on switching to that tier.

In the portal you can:

- Cancel the subscription. It ends at the end of the period you paid for, and your role and perks stay until then. Until it ends, you can renew it in the portal.
- Switch between Supporter and Supporter+. Switching to Supporter+ charges the difference for the rest of the period at once. Switching to Supporter starts at your next renewal.
- Change your card and see your invoices.

The tab loads again the next time you open it. The launcher doesn't start a second subscription while you have one.
