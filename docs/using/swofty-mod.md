# In-game addon settings

To change an addon's settings without leaving the game, open **ADDON SETTINGS**. SwoftyMod adds this screen to the home menu, directly under **Career**.

## Open it

1. Press **Play** in the launcher to launch the game.
2. On the home menu, select **ADDON SETTINGS**, directly under **Career**. You can also press **F9** (**R3** on a controller).

You'll also see a panel on the home screen confirming you launched with Swofty Launcher for F1 25.

## What's listed

**ADDON SETTINGS** lists a tile for every enabled addon, mod and track in the active profile, in a row per folder of the launcher's Addons page: **Built In**, the categories, such as **Tracks**, and your own folders, in the order the Addons page has them. Each row is labelled with its folder's name. Addons in no folder go in an **Addons** row, and mods in a **Mods** row. A track addon has one tile, which shows its track. Each tile shows its name and icon; the highlighted tile also shows its author, version and description, cut short with "..." when it runs past the tile.

Two rows fit on screen, with the next one showing below them. Moving down past them slides the rows up by one, and moving up slides them back. Three tiles of a row fit across: moving past the right edge slides that row along by a tile, and moving left slides it back. Moving up or down lands on the same column of the next row, or its last tile. Coming back from an addon's page returns to the tile you opened, with the rows slid to show it.

A page holds up to 24 tiles. With more, the list continues on further pages, and the title shows the page, such as **PAGE 1/3**. The last row of a page ends with **NEXT PAGE** and, from the second page on, **PREVIOUS PAGE**; each shows the first names on that page. **Back** on a later page returns to the page before it, with **NEXT PAGE** selected.

## Change a setting

1. On **ADDON SETTINGS**, select the tile for the addon you want to change.
2. Move to a setting row on the addon's page.
3. Press **Left** or **Right** to change the value.

An addon's page shows 18 rows. When it has more, moving past the bottom row slides the list up by a row, and moving past the top row slides it back down.

Only addons have settings. A mod's page shows its id, author and version; a track's page shows its track code, author and version. For an addon with no settings, you'll see its id and version. That makes **ADDON SETTINGS** the one place in game to see everything a profile turns on.

## When changes take effect

Your changes save to the active profile, but when they take effect depends on the setting: a live setting reaches the addon at once, while a database setting applies after you restart the game. You can also find these settings in the launcher, in each addon's row on the **Addons** page.

If the game build isn't recognised, the **ADDON SETTINGS** entry may be unavailable.

## Era picker

SwoftyMod splits the era picker of **Grand Prix** and **Time Trial** into two rows. The top row, **Built In**, holds the game's own eras, such as F1 2026 and F2 2026. The bottom row, **Addons**, holds the eras that addons add. Each row scrolls sideways on its own; press **Up** or **Down** to move between them.

Some addons, such as imported season mods, replace this menu with their own copy. When SwoftyMod can't edit that copy, the menu stays as the addon made it, without the Addons row, while the addon is on. The build shows a warning that names the addon.
