# Swofty Launcher 1.9

Swofty Launcher 1.9 brings a redesigned launcher and Time Trial leaderboards for addon content, with replays of every lap on them.

## What's new

- **A redesigned launcher.** Tabs along the top for Home, Library, Discover, Online and Support.
- **Time Trial leaderboards.** The new **Online** tab has a leaderboard for every addon season and track, with a season ranking, driver search and each driver's lap history. Set a lap in Time Trial on addon content while signed in to the launcher and it goes on the board. Each lap shows the assists and input device it was driven with, and you can filter boards by them.
- **In the game.** Time Trial on addon content shows your launcher name and tells you where you placed when you set a personal best.
- **Lap replays.** A personal best saves the game's own replay of the lap and uploads it with the lap. **Add to game** on a leaderboard row puts that replay into the game's Theatre, so you can watch any lap on the board in the game's replay viewer.
- **A new main menu.** The game opens on tiles for Driver Career, My Team, Grand Prix and Time Trial.
- **Support page.** It shows the monthly goal, the supporter tiers and their perks, and current supporters.
- **Español.** The launcher is now available in Spanish (1.9.1), under Settings > Language.

## Fixed in 1.9.1

- **Play** works again when F1 25 has moved to another folder, for example after a fresh Steam install.
- Going **Back** on the **Online** tab no longer leaves a season or driver page loading forever.
