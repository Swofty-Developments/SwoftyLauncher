# DMCA notice

This form sends a copyright takedown notice under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)) to Swofty Developments about an addon in the Addon Browser. Use it if you own the copyright in a work, or are authorised to act for the owner, and an addon uses that work without permission.

A notice needs every field below except the optional ones. Swofty Developments may send a copy of the notice, including your name and contact details, to the addon's publisher so they can respond.

::: warning
Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly misrepresents that material infringes can be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees.
:::
