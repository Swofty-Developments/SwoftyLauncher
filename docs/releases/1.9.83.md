# 1.9.83

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.83 gives a livery pack's car archives the handling and the base car of the car that wears them.

## Add-ons

- A livery's car archive, such as each car of SWOFTY 2012 Real Liveries, gets the handling of the car that wears it at build: the gears, engine, aero and every other value the season's handling records set. When the livery pack has handling records for its own archives, those apply instead. Until now a car with a livery pack on drove the handling its archive shipped with. See [A livery's archive](/std/systems/car-physics#a-livery-s-archive).
- A livery's car archive also names its season's base car, so Time Trial with equal performance drives the season's base car and not F1 25's 2025 car.
- The launcher keeps each changed livery archive in its cache and makes it again only when the archive or the handling changes.
- A handling record that names a file no `file.put` op puts in the game folder and no livery names is a warning on the build. Until now nothing was applied and nothing said so.
