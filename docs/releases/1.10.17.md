# 1.10.17

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.17 adds **Forget** on the Saves page for careers that are **Missing** or **Deleted in game**, and stops the menu beside **Play** being drawn under an ad.

## Saves

- A career that is **Missing** or **Deleted in game** on the **Saves** page now has a **Forget** button, which takes it off the list. It deletes no save: the versions that hold the career keep it, its addon data stays, and the career is listed again when the game's save holds it again. A forgotten career no longer shows in the careers lost banner. **Forget** needs F1 25 closed. See [Saves](/using/career-saves).

## Fixes

- The menu beside **Play** was drawn under the ad slot. Ads now hide while any menu or dropdown list is open.
