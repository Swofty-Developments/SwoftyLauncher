# 1.9.31

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.31 keeps My Team cars from addons safe in your saves and stops left-out tracks from blocking Play.

## Careers

- A My Team career whose car comes from an addon that isn't loaded, such as a More Engine Suppliers car with More Engine Suppliers off, loads with a stock car standing in, and the save keeps your original car. Until now the launcher left that car out of the career, and the next save dropped it for good. Turn the addon back on and the career has its own car again.
- A My Team car with no base car can take its own addon vehicle as its base, so it gets past the Team HQ loading screen when its addon is on.

## Tracks

- When the game's 64-track limit leaves a track out of a build, another addon's change to that track is skipped with a warning instead of stopping Play. Until now only the track addon's own changes were skipped, so a season or career addon that touches the track, such as Classic Career, refused Play.
