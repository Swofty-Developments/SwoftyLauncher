# 1.9.12

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.12 returns you to the mode you actually drove after a session.

## Fixes

- After you open Grand Prix from the main menu, switch to the Time Trial tab and drive a Time Trial, the game now returns to the Time Trial picker instead of New Grand Prix. The same goes the other way round, from the Time Trial card to a Grand Prix.
