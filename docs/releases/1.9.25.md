# 1.9.25

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.25 lets My Team careers that lost their team car race again.

## Fixes

- A My Team career that lost its team car crashes no more when a session starts. 1.9.21 left every My Team car out of a career while it loaded (1.9.22 still did for cars from More Engine Suppliers), and a career saved then kept no car for your team, so it crashed going into practice, qualifying or a race. When a session starts and a team's car is missing, the launcher now rebuilds it from one of the game's own My Team cars, and the career saves it. `loader.log` names the car it used.
- The rebuilt car is a stock My Team car, so its engine or name can differ from the one you had. To get your exact car back instead, restore the career's save from before you first played on 1.9.21 or 1.9.22: open **Profiles > Saves**, pick the newest **Before Play** or **After Play** version from before then, and select **Restore**. A restore still works after the car was rebuilt.
