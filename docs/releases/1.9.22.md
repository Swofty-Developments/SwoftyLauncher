# 1.9.22

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.22 fixes My Team careers that 1.9.21 broke, and three crashes in Time Trial and Grand Prix.

## Fixes

- My Team careers load as they did before 1.9.21. 1.9.21 left some of the game's own My Team cars out of a career while it loaded, such as `myteam_mercedes` or `myteam_audi_2026`, so the career crashed later or showed the wrong car models. The launcher now only leaves out a car that a known addon added, such as an engine from More Engine Suppliers that is turned off, and loads every other car as the game does. If a My Team career was saved while 1.9.21 was running and still crashes or shows the wrong car, restore its save from before you first played on 1.9.21: open **Profiles > Saves**, pick a **Before Play** version from before then, and select **Restore**.
- Opening Time Trial from the home screen after a Grand Prix with a season addon no longer closes the game. The Time Trial screen could pick an entrant list with no leaderboard, which the game doesn't allow. It now starts with no reference lap instead, and `loader.log` names the list.
- A Grand Prix no longer crashes as it opens when the track you last raced it on is no longer in the game, such as a track addon you've since turned off. That Grand Prix now moves to Melbourne, the same fallback a session load uses.
- Time Trial replays in the game's Theatre play with **In-game leaderboards** off. With that setting off, the replay loaded without its setup, so the game built a Time Trial session without your car and crashed. A Time Trial replay whose setup can't be found is now refused instead of loading a broken session.
