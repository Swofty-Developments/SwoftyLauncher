# 1.9.89

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.89 stops a career crashing at the first session of a race weekend that starts inside the season break.

## Fixes

- A Driver Career round that starts inside the season break no longer leaves for Team HQ as practice 1 opens and crashes at `F1_25.exe+0x1f8be5c`. The stock 2026 season's break runs 15 days from 27 July, and Historical Calendar 1.4.8 has a round on 30 July in 2016, 2017, 2019 to 2022, 2024 and 2025, such as round 12 of a 2016 Progressive Career. As the session opens, the core sets the season break to ignored, the race weekend runs, and `loader.log` says `[career] season break over race weekend`. That season has no break after the round. See [Weekend diagnostics](/reference/std/career#weekend-diagnostics).
