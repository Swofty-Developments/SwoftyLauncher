# 1.9.4

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.4 fixes Grand Prix saves, Split Screen and crashes from 1.9, and the slowdowns that were left in races.

## Fixes

- The **Grand Prix** card on the main menu opens the game's Grand Prix screen again: your saved seasons are listed so you can carry on, and a new Grand Prix gets a save slot, so it saves. Since 1.9 the card went straight to a new Grand Prix, so saved seasons weren't offered, races didn't save, and the game sometimes dropped you back into an old session. Your saved seasons were kept in your profile save the whole time.
- **Split Screen** is back on the main menu, between Time Trial and Theatre. The 1.9 main menu had left out the game's Local Multiplayer tile, which is the only way into split screen.
- A Grand Prix no longer crashes on the loading screen when the track it raced last time belongs to an addon that's now off or in another profile. It starts at Melbourne instead, and you pick the track as usual.
- With **Time Trial boards** on, races and other sessions no longer slow down. The leaderboard checks that ran several hundred times a second in every session now run only in a Time Trial session, at most twice a second.
- **Move…** for your addons and saves no longer fails with "state\registry.log differs after the copy". The launcher's own logs grow while it copies, and the move now allows for that; your addons, profiles and saves are still checked exactly.
- On the EA app copy, Time Trial no longer gets stuck on checking privileges with **In-game leaderboards** on.
