# 1.10.61

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.61 catches a crash that could end a new My Team career right after the team creation screens.

## Fixes

- Starting a new My Team career: right after the team creation screens, the game sometimes reads the player's part-rate record when there isn't one, and that crashed the game. The launcher now catches that read and lets the game carry on without the player's part rates, as the game itself does when a record is empty. When it happens, the log gets a `[myteam] part rates` line saying which entrant was missing, and Send Logs includes it.

## For add-on makers

- `site_Car_ContactModify_AssistCheck`, the assist check inside `Car_Contact_ModifyVehicle`, now exports as an experimental binding.
- `site_Car_ContactModify_Exit` and `Car_WheelPart_Detach` are L2 now, with corrected notes and prototype (`void Car_WheelPart_Detach(void *part, uint8_t notify)`). They export once a second contributor agrees.
