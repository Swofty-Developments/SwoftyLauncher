# 1.10.15

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.15 runs the rules of a career season's box in the career, gives the EA app copy its own plain copy of every career, and stops the careers lost banner after switching between the Steam and EA app copies.

## Careers

- A Driver Career or My Team career whose season names an add-on's box, such as a Progressive Career season, now runs that box's rules in the career. At each session load the box's DRS, tyre warmers, mandatory tyre change, race time limit, points, refuelling and race fuel, grid and qualifying tyre rules apply. The career keeps its own weekend format, safety car, formation lap, penalties and corner cutting settings. A season of a stock box, of an add-on that is off, or whose year isn't known yet races under the game's rules, and unloading the career puts the game's rules back. This is experimental. `SWOFTY_CAREER_RULES=0` turns it off. See [The season's rules](/std/systems/career#the-season-s-rules).

## Fixes

- With **Use launcher saves** on, the EA app copy now keeps its own plain copy of every career, in `saves/<profile>/ea-plain`, apart from the Steam copy's in `saves/<profile>/plain`. A career one copy saved is no longer put back into the other copy's save, and the careers lost banner no longer shows after switching between the Steam and EA app copies.
- A profile played on both copies has its shared plain copies split once, at the first launcher start or **Play** of this version with F1 25 closed. Nothing is deleted: the generations the EA app copy wrote are copied into `ea-plain`, and their originals are kept in `plain/split-ea`. See [Splitting a shared store](/reference/saves#splitting-a-shared-store).

## Addon API

- `career::report_season` for a season that already started in the career, such as after a saved career loads, now gives the core the career's year without a second `career.season.start`. A career no add-on owns gets its year from the game the same way. An owner can report at the first idle Team HQ after each career load as well as at each season's start, and a resumed career then runs its season's rules. See [When a season starts](/std/systems/career#when-a-season-starts).
