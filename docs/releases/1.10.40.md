# 1.10.40

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.40 brings live race data to the EA app copy of the game, explains stalled offline play downloads, and adds detail to load-stall reports.

## Fixes

- On the EA app copy of F1 25, addons now get the grid, lap times, tyre wear and tyre age in races. Timing tower addons such as TV Timing Tower kept the game's own tower in races there, because the launcher only read that data on the Steam copy.
- When an offline play download stalls until the timeout, the error now leads with the likely cause, a virus scanner or web filter holding the download, instead of only reporting a timeout.

## Diagnostics

- When a session gets stuck while unloading, the load-stall report now names the step the game is waiting on, so these reports can be traced to their cause.
