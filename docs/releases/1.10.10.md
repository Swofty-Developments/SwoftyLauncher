# 1.10.10

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.10 fixes My Team's blank livery screens and the empty Career list on EA copies, gives every team in an 11- or 12-team season a garage in Time Trial, and fixes a crash on some AMD systems.

## Fixes

- My Team no longer opens with a blank garage and an empty Colour Scheme row when you start it from the Career list: the career's car group is selected from the list row (2025 and 2026 My Team), and `SWOFTY_RESUME_LIST=0` in `config.json` `launch.env` now reaches the launcher.
- In Time Trial, the 11th and 12th teams of a season with more teams than the track has garages, such as HRT in SWOFTY 2012, had no garage. Their car started at a random spot with no controls, and Restart Lap showed a panoramic view. A season add-on with more teams than a track has garages now gets a world with a garage for each team, in Time Trial and in Grand Prix.
- On some AMD systems the game crashed during a session after AMD's driver unloaded a Windows system file the launcher reads the GPU use from. The launcher now keeps that file loaded and guards its calls.
- The Career list no longer comes up empty on EA copies: it reads the save the session will load, and a vault restore or a save changed outside the launcher shows at the next Play.
- A track add-on whose AI data the game can't load (for example a corner starting on the start/finish gate) now stops the build with a message naming the add-on, and Send Logs names the track when the game crashes on it.
- A `car.profile` record whose `within` no session can match, such as two `content` entries that must both hold, applied nothing without a word. The core now logs it once, with the form that matches either.
