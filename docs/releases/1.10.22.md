# 1.10.22

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.22 makes the SWOFTY 2010, 2011 and 2012 Grand Prix boxes race all 24 cars.

## Fixes

- SWOFTY 2010, 2011 and 2012 Grand Prix boxes race all 24 cars, whatever other seasons are installed and in whatever order.

## Addon API

- A box whose `content.package` seats more than 22 drivers raises the grid to its seats when it is pressed, up to 24 cars. See [Pressing a box](/reference/std/era-picker#pressing-a-box).
