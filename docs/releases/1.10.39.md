# 1.10.39

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.39 fixes Grand Prix weekends on the reversed stock tracks, lets addons hide parts of the game's other screens, and gives addons the drivers' grid rundown portraits.

## Fixes

- A Grand Prix weekend at Austria Reverse, Silverstone Reverse or Zandvoort Reverse loads again. With no track addon on, the launcher stopped the weekend as "not a track of this build" and closed the game, although the three reversed layouts come with F1 25.

## Standard library

- Addons can hide parts of the game's other screens, such as the race winner and Driver of the Day graphics, while their own replacements are on. An addon opts in with `ui` entries in a yield record; nothing changes for addons that don't use them.
- Addons can show each driver's portrait from the game's pre-race grid rundown, through `live::start_portraits()`.
