# 1.10.60

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.60 fixes the HUD error that stopped Play in 1.10.59 for players with a HUD add-on, and the first-play setup no longer waits forever on the ads consent form.

## Fixes

- Play no longer stops with "the HUD elements do not compose: no game archive carries ..." when a HUD add-on, such as F1 Broadcast Overlay, uses one of the game's stock HUD files. This came in with 1.10.59.
- The ads step of the first-play setup moves on after 30 seconds when the consent form never answers, instead of staying stuck on it.
- The Showroom card on the main menu opens the Showroom through the same routes as the game's F1 World hub.
- After a session that ended normally, the problem notice says "Found in your last session" for what the launcher found (tyres, logos or saves), instead of calling it a crash.
- The game's own log lines are captured again on the current F1 25 version (1.0.145, Steam and EA), so Send Logs includes them.
- When Steam starts the game through the launcher and the start fails, the failure is now written to `state/wrapper-run.log` and included in Send Logs.
