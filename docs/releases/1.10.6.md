# 1.10.6

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.6 shows your own Time Trial times as soon as you sign in and marks your row on a board by your account.

## Fixes

- Time Trial showed your own best laps and gaps only after you had opened your own profile. They now load when you sign in.
- A Time Trial board marked your row by your display name, so another driver with the same name was marked instead, and your row was never pinned when it was outside the rows shown. The board now marks and pins the lap the server records as yours.
- The Time Trial filters ran past the page in a narrow window or a longer language. They now wrap onto their own lines.
- On a season page in a narrow window, a long record holder name ran into the drivers and laps column. It is now cut short.
