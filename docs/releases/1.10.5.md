# 1.10.5

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.5 fixes a crash after restoring a career mode copy and stops the V key opening the home screen video from another window.

## Fixes

- Restoring a career mode copy from Career Saves no longer closes the game at the next Play. A restore request that is left over after a crash is withdrawn and does not repeat.
- The home screen video no longer opens when the V key is pressed in another window.
