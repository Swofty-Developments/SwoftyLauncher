# 1.9.5

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.5 makes Time Trial laps count on the EA app copy and stops a crash when opening Theatre.

## Fixes

- On the EA app copy, Time Trial laps are recorded again and reach the leaderboards. Before, the launcher couldn't read the player's car on the EA build, so no lap was ever written or uploaded. Laps from the EA copy carry the car's speed, position, inputs and gears; a few values the launcher can only read on the Steam build, such as wheel slip and the car setup, are left out.
- Opening **Theatre** no longer crashes the game when your profile save holds a replay or highlight whose owner is gone. That item is listed without a save slot instead. The crash came from the game itself and happened before 1.9 too; the main menu's Theatre tile made it easier to reach.
