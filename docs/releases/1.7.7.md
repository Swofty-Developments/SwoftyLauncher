# 1.7.7

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.7.7 adds per-box garage art for season addons.

## Addons

- A season addon can give a team its own garage and pit lane art in one Grand Prix or Time Trial box with a `team.garage` record: Classic Seasons 2020 shows the Renault garage in 2020 colours, while other boxes keep their own garages. See [Garage art](/std/systems/garage).
