# 1.9.9

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.9 brings your saved Grand Prix seasons back to the main menu.

## Fixes

- The **Grand Prix** card on the main menu opens the game's Grand Prix screen the way F1 World does: your saved seasons are listed so you can **Continue** one, or start a new Grand Prix, which saves as you race. Since 1.9 the card either skipped the saved seasons (1.9.0 to 1.9.3 and 1.9.6 to 1.9.8) or crashed on them (1.9.4 and 1.9.5). Your saved seasons were kept in your save the whole time.
- **Back** on that screen returns to the main menu, and leaving a Grand Prix session returns to the saved seasons.
