# 1.10.4

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.4 loads careers the 1.10 career list refused.

## Fixes

- The career list refused careers whose car, team or driver an add-on adds in game through the core's registry, such as every Classic Seasons 2016 to 2024 car, with **Career can't load** and `Missing rows from <add-on>` while that add-on was on. The list now counts those rows, and such a career is `Ready`.
- A career whose team, car or driver row the list still cannot find shows a yellow `Mismatched rows from <add-on>` and opens **Add-ons do not match** with **Load Anyway** and **Back**. It said **Career can't load** and could not be loaded.
- A career in an add-on's own career mode whose name ends in a number, such as `driver_career_f2_26`, was sent to the game's 2026 career and the list said **Career can't load**. It now loads in its own mode.
- The list's yellow status reads `Mismatched`, and a career saved with another version of an add-on says `Mismatched version of <add-on>`. `Missing` now only means an add-on that is off or not installed.
