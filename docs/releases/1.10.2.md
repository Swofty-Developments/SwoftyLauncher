# 1.10.2

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.2 fills the career list on the EA app copy and words version differences in **Add-ons do not match**.

## Fixes

- On the EA app copy, the career list opened with no careers, so you had to press **New Career** and resume from the game's own screen. It now lists the careers of your launcher save.
- **Add-ons do not match** said `Missing: <addon> <version>` for an add-on that is on but at another version from the one the career was saved with. It now says `<addon> (saved with <version>, installed <version>)`, and lists add-ons that are off or not installed under `Missing`.

## Addon API

- The addon binding `Game_PitStopActionObserver_SetTransitionSequence` is removed from the addon API: its notes' exe claim is wrong: the only refuel strings are parts of the EnsureFuelLoad race objective names, and there is no fuelling string.
- The addon binding `Ne_Property_SetS64IfChanged` is removed from the addon API: renamed: every caller sets a UIDBRef weak object reference through it and the compare is object identity, not a numeric S64 compare. Use `Ne_Property_SetUIDBRefIfChanged` instead.
- The addon binding `Ne_Std_ThrowException` is removed from the addon API: renamed: it is std::_Throw_bad_optional_access, which only raises std::bad_optional_access and ends the process in a fast fail. Use `Ne_Std_ThrowBadOptionalAccess` instead.
