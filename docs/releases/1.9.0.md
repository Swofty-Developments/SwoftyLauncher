# 1.9.0

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.0 adds Time Trial leaderboards for addon seasons, with replays of the laps on them.

## Time Trial leaderboards

- The new **Online** tab lists a leaderboard for every addon season and track, with a season ranking, driver search and each driver's lap history. A lap you set in Time Trial on addon content is uploaded when you are signed in to the launcher.
- Each lap shows the assists and input device it was driven with, and boards can be filtered by them.
- In the game, Time Trial on addon content shows your launcher name and tells you your position on the board when you set a personal best.
- A track counts for the season ranking once three drivers have set a lap on it.

## Lap replays

- A personal best in Time Trial on addon content saves the game's own replay of the lap, titled with the lap time, and uploads it with the lap.
- **Add to game** on a leaderboard row puts that lap's replay into the game's Theatre. Picking it there plays the lap in the game's replay viewer, and leaving the viewer returns to the menus.
- The recording is restarted between laps so a saved replay holds the lap and not the whole session. The pause menu's Instant Replay in Time Trial on addon content reaches back to the last restart.

## Main menu

- The game's main menu opens on launcher tiles for Driver Career, My Team, Grand Prix and Time Trial, and the pickers return to it.
- Entering Time Trial while signed out of the launcher says that laps will not be uploaded.

## Launcher

- The top navigation has tabs for the launcher's pages, including **Support**.
- The Support page replaces the donate button: it shows the monthly goal, the supporter tiers with their perks, and current supporters.
- A banned account is told the reason when it signs in.

## Fixes

- The in-game leaderboard tabs that need the publisher's online service no longer take input on addon content.
- The account-state popups are not shown for addon content.
