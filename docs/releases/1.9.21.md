# 1.9.21

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.21 fixes two crashes in careers.

## Fixes

- F1 25 no longer crashes at start-up, on the title screen or when you continue a My Team career, when the career was made with an engine or car that an addon adds, such as one from More Engine Suppliers, and that addon is now off or doesn't add it any more. The game left that car out of its data and then looked it up anyway when it loaded the career. The launcher now skips that one car and loads the rest of the career. `loader.log` names the car and the addon; turn the addon back on, or add the engine back in its settings, to get the car back.
- Starting a career from an addon's career tile no longer crashes the game when the game hasn't loaded that career's driver line-up. The launcher now checks the line-up against the lists the game actually loaded, and turns the career away with a reason in `loader.log` instead.
