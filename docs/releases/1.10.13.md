# 1.10.13

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.13 guards a crash in F1 25's own animation code, seen in the cockpit steering wheel animation.

## Fixes

- F1 25 could crash in its own animation code when an animation was sampled at a time that is not a number. Every report so far is the cockpit steering wheel animation, at a session start, after a flashback, after the podium cutscene or on resume. What makes that time stop being a number is not known. The launcher now gives such an animation its first frame instead of letting the game fault, and the steering wheel animation holds that frame while its time stays not a number. `SWOFTY_ANIM_CLIP_TIME_GUARD=0` turns this off.
- The crash screen now names a crash in F1 25's own animation code. It says this is a game fault and asks you to press Send Logs.
- The crash screen now recognises the tyre wear crash at one more of the game's tyre curve reads, and on EA 1.0.141.
