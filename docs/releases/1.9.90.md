# 1.9.90

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.90 makes Play's track limit check take under a second with 22 or more track addons on.

## Fixes

- With 22 or more added tracks on, Play built the whole profile once just to find the tracks past the game's 64-track limit, before the real build started. On some PCs that check took 9 to 15 minutes on every Play. The check now fits the tracks of the on addons' op files and track records to the game's track lists the way the build fits them, without building, and takes under a second. It leaves out the same tracks, and the **Too many tracks** warning and the Addons page's **Left out** markers are unchanged. The app log's `[play] track limit` line now says `the check fitted their tracks to the lists` with its time. See [Installing tracks](/reference/installing-tracks).
- The track limit check no longer fails with `Simplified for Swofty did not answer within 5 s` and skips the **Too many tracks** warning. It starts no addon programs.
