# 1.9.26

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.26 finishes the repair of My Team careers that lost their team car in 1.9.21 or 1.9.22, and makes Time Trial laps save on EA app copies under Program Files.

## Fixes

- A My Team career whose team car was lost now loads again, wherever it used to crash: opening the career, Team HQ, the car reveal or a session. The launcher now rebuilds every missing My Team car as the career loads, for you and your teammate, the way the game builds a new team's car, from one of the game's own My Team cars. Before, 1.9.25 rebuilt the car only when a session started, which was too late for careers that crashed earlier.
- Team HQ no longer stays on the loading screen after 1.9.25 rebuilt your car. The car 1.9.25 rebuilt had no base car, which Team HQ needs; the launcher now gives it one when the career loads.
- `loader.log` has one line per My Team career load that says how many of the career's cars were missing and rebuilt.
- The rebuilt car is a stock My Team car, so its engine or name can differ from yours. To get your exact car back, restore the career's save from before you first played on 1.9.21 or 1.9.22: open **Profiles > Saves**, pick the newest **Before Play** or **After Play** version from before then, and select **Restore**.
- Time Trial laps, their replays and the in-game leaderboards work on the EA app copy installed under `Program Files`. The game couldn't write lap files into its own folder there, so no lap was saved or uploaded. Laps, replays and leaderboard requests now go through the launcher's data folder for every copy.
