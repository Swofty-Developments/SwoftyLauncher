# 1.9.36

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.36 builds addons' team logos into every copy of the game's logo lists.

## Addons

- When the game's archives carry different copies of a team logo list (for example after another addon rewrote one of them), the logos a season addon adds go into every copy at the same place instead of none of them. Until now a mismatch between the copies left out every new logo from that list, such as the F1 2010 addon's broadcast badges.
- A copy the build can't read is left as it is with one line in the log naming its archive, and the other copies still get the logos.
