# 1.10.11

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.11 fixes careers stuck at Team HQ after a season break, Play failing after a game update left a menu file patched, and the rev lights of V8 seasons in Time Trial.

## Fixes

- After a race weekend that fell inside the season break, such as round 12 with F2 Revamp, Team HQ's Advance stayed off for the rest of the break and the career could not go on. The launcher now moves that break so it ends the day before, and Advance works again. A career already stuck this way is moved the next time it opens at Team HQ. `SWOFTY_HUB_BREAK_FIX=0` turns this off.
- In Time Trial, every car's rev lights followed the 2025 Mercedes engine, so the lights of an 18,000 rpm V8 car, such as in SWOFTY 2010 to 2013, filled near 12,000 rpm. A season now shows its own engine's rev lights on the wheel and the HUD. `SWOFTY_REV_LIGHTS=0` turns this off.
- Play no longer fails every time with "menus: the tooltips already have mod_menu" after a game update leaves a menu file patched: the launcher puts back the clean original, or tells you to repair the game if no clean copy is on record.
