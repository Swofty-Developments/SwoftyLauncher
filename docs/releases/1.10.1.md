# 1.10.1

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.1 makes the career list load the career you pick.

## Fixes

- Picking an F1 25 2025 career, or a career of an addon's career mode such as Progressive, on the career list opened the game's own Resume Career screen instead of loading it. Pressing a save there could load a different career. The list now loads the career you pick. When the list can't open a career, the Resume Career screen shows **Career can't load**; pick the career on that screen.
- A full drive during a session no longer crashes the launcher, and the game folder is put back after F1 25 closes.
