# 1.9.7

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.7 fixes Grand Prix races on older tracks and a track that stopped Play, and narrows the Time Trial pages to the stock game and the SWOFTY seasons.

## Fixes

- A Grand Prix on an older track that fills one of the game's unused track slots, such as Portimão or Sochi, starts on that track again instead of at Melbourne. 1.9.4's fix for a Grand Prix on a removed track also caught tracks whose addon was on.
- A track addon with an energy recovery (ERS) setting the launcher doesn't know yet, such as Qiddiya 1.38.0's DeployLimitMGUK, no longer stops every Play. The unknown setting is left out and the rest of the track's settings apply.

## Changes

- The Time Trial pages show the stock game and the SWOFTY seasons only: the Online tab, the board and season pickers, your season standings and lap lists, and recently viewed seasons. Only those count toward the global ranking. Laps on other seasons still upload and stay on their own boards.
