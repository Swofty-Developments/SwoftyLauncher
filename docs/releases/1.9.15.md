# 1.9.15

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.15 fixes Time Trial laps on the EA app copy of the game.

## Fixes

- On the EA app copy of the game, Time Trial laps now record whether the session was dry or wet, taken from the game's own Time Trial board for the session. Before, the lap had no weather and the leaderboards turned it down with "the lap has no usable board".
- Smoother frame rates in career and Grand Prix with the in-game leaderboards and Time Trial boards on.
