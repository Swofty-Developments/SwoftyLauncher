# 1.9.35

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.35 stops a race crash when a car retires and makes stuck My Team careers easier to diagnose.

## Races

- A race no longer crashes when a car retires and F1 25 can't find its driver (in Sess_InGameRace_Update, as the retirement message names the driver). The message shows no name for that car instead, and the loader log names the car and the driver it couldn't find once. If that crash still happens, the crash report says a car in your race has a driver F1 25 can't find.

## Careers

- When a My Team career loads and the launcher's car repair stops before it lists the career's cars, the loader log says why in one line: which step of the game's championship data it couldn't read, or that no entrant is on a My Team car, with what it read. Until now the repair stopped without a word, so a Send Logs from a stuck My Team career on the EA app copy didn't show whether it saw the cars at all.
