# 1.10.7

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.7 lets two season add-ons with their own HUD be on in one profile, and stops add-on patch updates marking careers as mismatched.

## Fixes

- With two season add-ons that each bring a timing tower and onboard HUD on together, such as SWOFTY 2010 and SWOFTY 2011, Play failed with "lap_counter.idf is wrapped by another record already". Both now build, each season shows its own HUD in its races, and the stock HUD shows in every other race.
- With both of those add-ons on, a race of one season showed the stock tower and dial under its own. The stock tower, dial, lap counter and flag message now hide while either season's HUD is showing.
- A career saved with an add-on at one patch version showed as `Mismatched` once that add-on updated to a newer patch, such as 1.0.5 to 1.0.6. A newer patch now counts as `Ready`, and the career records the new version the next time the game saves it.
- The notifications bell no longer overlaps the donate button when the launcher window is not maximised.
