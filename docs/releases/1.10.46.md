# 1.10.46

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.46 keeps slow career loads running and explains a career crash caused by an oversized grid.

## Fixes

- A Driver Career or My Team career opened from the menu long after the menu appeared is no longer closed by the game's freeze check when it takes more than 30 seconds to load on a slower PC. The launcher now holds the game's freeze check for up to 5 minutes from the moment the career starts loading, as it already did during start-up.
- Time Trial counts each car without a vehicle of its own, such as the cars of the stock F2 2024 list, in the number of cars it expects.

## Diagnostics

- A career that crashes the moment its first session starts because its grid has more than 22 cars now shows a known problem on the crash screen and in Send Logs. It names the number of cars and says to start a new career with the stock line-ups or keep 2 drivers per team.
