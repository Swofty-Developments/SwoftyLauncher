# 1.10.65

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.65 fixes new careers that the resume list could not open.

## Fixes

- A new career, such as a Progressive Career, could show "Career can't load" over an empty Continue screen when you tried to resume it. The launcher kept two records for the career, one from before its first save and one for its save slot, and the save slot's record had no data. The launcher now joins them at the first save, and repairs careers already split this way the next time you start the game.
