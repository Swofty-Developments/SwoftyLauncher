# 1.9.41

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.41 stops a start-up crash from addon options that add races at a missing track, and gives addons a new game binding.

## Addon options

- When a toggle in an addon's settings adds rounds at a track that isn't in the build, the build treats that toggle as off and logs a warning naming the addon, the option and the track. Until now the game crashed at start-up. That was the crash with F2 Revamp's Portimao or Istanbul Park option on and that track not installed.
- Your saved setting isn't changed: turn on the track's addon in the same profile and the option applies again.

## For addon authors

- New binding `Game_Fill_PerformanceComparison`: the game's fill of Team HQ's vehicle performance comparison, before it sorts the teams and builds the panel.
