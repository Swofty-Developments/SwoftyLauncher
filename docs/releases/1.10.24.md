# 1.10.24

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.24 stops Simplified for Swofty warning that it needs one of its addons.

## Fixes

- Simplified for Swofty no longer warns that it needs one of its addons: an addon with its own pages, such as the Simplified Importer, works on its own.

## Addon API

- An addon with its own launcher pages or imports (`launcher.sections` or `launcher.imports`) never gets the **needs one of its addons** warning, even when none of the addons that need it are on.
