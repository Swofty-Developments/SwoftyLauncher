# 1.9.11

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.11 fixes Grand Prix for players with no track addon, trims Time Trial replays to your lap and makes the core cheaper every frame.

## Fixes

- A Grand Prix on a stock track loads again when no track addon is on. In 1.9.10 the game closed at the loading screen with "the saved weekend's track ... is not a track of this build".

## Performance

- The core's per-frame work runs once a frame instead of several times a frame. In Time Trial it now costs about a third of what it did.
- With in-game leaderboards on, the check of the game's ghost download that ran every half second in Time Trial is about 66 times cheaper. On some PCs it took over 100 ms and showed as a stutter.

## Time Trial

- The replay recording starts again at the line after each lap that isn't a new best, so the replay of your next best lap starts just before that lap instead of holding the laps before it.
- Your first lap's replay no longer holds the out-lap, and the recording also starts again at the line after a lap that was thrown away (the car was reset or moved, or stood still).

## Crash reports

- When the game hangs and logs its own deadlock warning, Send Logs now includes what every thread was doing.
