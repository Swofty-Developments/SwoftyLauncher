# 1.9.11

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.11 trims Time Trial replays to your lap.

## Time Trial

- The replay recording starts again at the line after each lap that isn't a new best, so the replay of your next best lap starts just before that lap instead of holding the laps before it.
