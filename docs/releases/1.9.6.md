# 1.9.6

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.6 fixes two crashes.

## Fixes

- Pressing the **Grand Prix** card on the main menu no longer crashes the game. 1.9.4 made the card open the game's Grand Prix saves screen, and opened from the main menu that screen crashes the game for many players. The card opens the new Grand Prix picker again, as it did in 1.9.3. Your saved Grand Prix seasons are still in your save.
- The game no longer crashes about 15 seconds after it starts when RivaTuner Statistics Server (it comes with MSI Afterburner) is running. Since 1.9 the launcher hooked the game's screen for the main menu panel and video card even with no overlay addon on, which collided with RivaTuner's own hooks. With RivaTuner running and no overlay addon on, the main menu panel and video card are left out; the home tiles still work.
