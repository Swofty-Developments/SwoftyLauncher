# 1.9.33

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.9.33 makes the My Team car checks work on the EA app copy.

## Careers

- On the EA app copy, the launcher now reads the game's entrant and car tables at the right place. Until now it read the place they sit on the Steam copy, so on EA the career-load repair, keeping an addon car whose addon is off, the era picker's checks and the career content checks saw no cars at all.
- A My Team car whose base names a car that isn't loaded gets the stock car of its own name as its base, the same as a car with no base. The loader log lists each My Team car with its base car's name.
