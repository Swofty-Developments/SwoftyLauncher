# 1.10.9

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.9 brings back the season break fix for career rounds, flags a save that is stuck at the start of a race weekend, names two new known problems in crash reports, and counts only uncached files in the free space check at Play.

## Careers

- The stock season break that overlaps a race weekend is handled again. A Driver Career round that starts inside the season break, such as a Historical Calendar round on 30 July, again runs its first session instead of leaving for Team HQ as practice 1 opens. This fix from 1.9.89 had stopped applying in 1.10.0. `loader.log` says `[career] season break over race weekend` when it runs. See [Weekend diagnostics](/reference/std/career#weekend-diagnostics).

## Career Saves

- A save with a career left inside a race weekend before any session of it ran opens Team HQ with no Advance. The Career Saves page and the Addons page now show a banner for it that names the round and the track, with **Restore** of the newest earlier version, **Review the restore** and **Dismiss**. The save as it is stays in Versions, and the launcher changes nothing until you choose. With no earlier version the banner says so. See [A save stuck in a started race weekend](/reference/saves#a-save-stuck-in-a-started-race-weekend).

## Play

- The free space check at Play counts only the files the game drive copy doesn't hold yet, plus 2 GB to spare, instead of the size of the whole profile. A profile whose files are already in the copy is no longer refused with `not enough free space on <drive>`. When the copy has too little room, the check counts copying the files straight into the game folder. See [Free space](/reference/restore#free-space).

## Send Logs

- **Your career is stuck at the start of a race weekend** shows when a session ends with its career in a race weekend that started with no session run. It names the round and the track and points to **Restore** on the Career Saves page. A session that then hit F1 25's error on closing shows it instead of **F1 25 hit an error as it closed**.
- **F1 25 left the race weekend for Team HQ as it opened** shows when F1 25 crashes as a session closes right after the calendar check sent the career to Team HQ. It names the round and, when the log names it, the calendar item the check put first, such as a season break over the race weekend.
