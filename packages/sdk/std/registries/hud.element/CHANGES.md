1.0: first version (launcher 1.9.0).
1.0 (additive, launcher 1.9.0): `scene` and `parent_ui` give a record a target besides the dial enum; `ui_menu_data.<name>` is a leaf path next to `view_model_data.<name>`.
1.1 (additive, launcher 1.9.10): rows and screens for the timing tower: `repeat` on a group with `{VV}`/`{V}` substitution, the `stack`, `centre` and `unit` kinds with `dir`, `gap`, `unit`, `size` and `texture`, text `literal_id` and `case: mixed`, the record fields `units`, `screen`, `show`, `also`, `stock_nodes` and `restyle`, bind `slot_var`, `screen` and `ty: si32`, sprite `texture` and `fit`, `editor.rows`. Dial fit: decision `initial`, variant `decisions` and `root_y`, `add` entries placed with `before` / `after` / `replace`, record `root_y`. Motion: `motion` on a group (`id`, `keys`, `curve`, `play_on`) for a timed slide or fade. Game values and stock parts: bind `path` (any game view-model path, `ty` si32/ui32/text/bool), element `colour` (a static colourOverride) and `hidden` as a static hide node, `stock_nodes.wrap` (a new null around a stock part another node targets), `also.<screen>.hide`, the record field `clock` (`output`, `start`), decisions `on` a path for screen records; a leaf declared `i32` binds si32 (the core writes int leaves as signed ints).
1.2 (additive, launcher 1.9.55): variant `stock` and `stock_parts` over the record's, so a variant behind a `switch` can hide its dial's stock parts while the base keeps its own (`park` is refused on a variant with a switch).
1.3 (additive, launcher 1.9.61): motion `kind: turn` with `play_to` (a view_model int leaf) and `steps` (2 to 64), keys `[time, degrees]`, for a needle; motion `kind` (slide, fade, turn); `play_on` is no longer required by the schema (a turn has none; the composer still requires it on a slide or fade).
1.4 (additive, launcher 1.9.75): record `switch` on a dial record, the same bind as a variant's: the record shows while the leaf is on and, with stock `hide`, the dial's stock parts show while it is off; a variant without its own switch inherits it. The switch's `default` (a bool) is the state while the leaf is absent, for the record and the stock parts alike (it was ignored on a variant switch: the record always started shown).
1.5 (additive, launcher 1.10.34): record `yield` (`parts`, `while`) in place of `dial`, `scene` or `screen`: the named parts (the dials, the timing boards, the lap counter, the flag box, the notifications, the session timer, the minimap) are hidden whole, stock and every record on them, while the leaf is on.
1.6 (additive, launcher 1.10.38): a yield record's `stock_nodes` entries with `ui` hide elements of a game screen other than the HUD template (`screens/broadcast_persistent.ui`, `screens/persistent.ui` …) while the yield's leaf or the entry's own `while` is on: `ui`, `template` (a node path of that screen), `scene` (required), `hide` and `wrap` as for the HUD template; an entry that does not fit the screen is left out with a build line. `yield.parts` may be empty when the record has such entries.
