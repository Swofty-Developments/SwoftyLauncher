## Question
Does a career save taken during the overridden weekend (between sessions and through the mid-session save) store the override vehicle ref in the weekend's session copies, and what does a reload do with and without the season add-on installed?

## Hypothesis
The session copies are saved with the weekend. With the restore before `career.saving` the save holds the stock refs and reloads cleanly. Without it, a reload without the add-on resolves the ref to null: the entrant is skipped in BuildSessionSettings (missing car) and the unchecked lookup in 0x142458710 may crash at session enter.
