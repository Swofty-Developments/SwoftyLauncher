# T260 How long does a stock pit stop stand, and does it equal the crew training target time?

- Requested by: engine-re, 2026-11-18
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
For one race pit stop of the player car and one AI car: the stationary time (pit status 4 InPitBox until status 5 InPitBox_CrewCompleted), compared with the target time `Game_PitCrewTraining_GetStopTargetTime` returns for that stop.

## Hypothesis
Static reading (game.md Inbox, E2 refuel round 2026-11-18):
- `Game_PitLane_GetPitStopTimeRatio_Thunk` 0x1434d0cb0 returns N / T and writes N. N is the mean of a pit_lane config pair (about 2.37 s stock); T comes from 0x141036ba0.
- `Game_PitStopActionObserver_SetTransitionSequence` picks the 21 or 258 sequence family and stores crew speed = 2.1 / T or 2.58 / T at *(pitlane+0x68+car*0x10)+0 (0x1403bda82).
- The crew sequence then takes T seconds of crew work. Its SendToTrack message moves the car from status 4 to 5 (`Game_PitLane_OnGarageCrewMessage` 0x1434cebe0).
- So the stationary time is T plus a fixed lead-in before the sequence Start event, and the failsafe rec+0xe8 (longest sequence length + 1 s) does not fire.
