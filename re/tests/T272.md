## Hypothesis
Car_GearBox_UpdateShiftTimersAndFaults adds (rate - limiter) / (limiter x MaxLimiterRateFactor) x OverrevvingDamageScale x dt x CarLive+0x9f30 while over the limiter and not slipping; Car_Engine_UpdateWear adds WearICERate x WearICERevsSpline(rpm) x dt/3600 x scales. With the stock VTF rates the per-lap ICE rise is small; the team VTF decides how large the over-rev term is.
