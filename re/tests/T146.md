# T146 Does the track acclimatisation penalty guard keep Driver Career practice at an imported track running when the car gets a track limits warning or penalty?

- Requested by: acclimpenalty, 2026-10-30
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
With the track acclimatisation penalty guard in core, does a Driver Career practice session at an imported community track (Hockenheim, Sochi or Delhi) keep running when the player's car gets its first track limits warning or penalty (the crash `ACCESS_VIOLATION reading 0xc4 at F1_25.exe+0x29a0634`, 3 to 12 minutes into FP1)?

## Hypothesis
The Track Acclimatisation programme's targets (self+0x350..+0x358) stay empty at a track without CornerData (`Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_CopyTargets`). `Game_PracticeProgramme_HandlePenaltyIssued` runs slot +0x1a8 for every programme on `penalty_issued_message` for the car, and `Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_FlagCurrentTarget` indexes entry [+0x328] without the empty check, so the first penalty or warning faults at 0x1429a0634. The guard (`site_Game_AcclimPenalty_Entry`, `game.acclim_penalty_entry`) continues at the function's ret (`site_Game_AcclimPenalty_Return`, `game.acclim_penalty_return`) when the vector is empty, so the session goes on with the programme inert. At a stock track the vector holds targets and the game's code runs, so a penalty still marks the current target.

## Promotes
- `site_Game_AcclimPenalty_Entry`, `site_Game_AcclimPenalty_Return`, `Game_PracticeTrackAcclimatisation_FlagCurrentTarget`: L2 -> L3 on the build the run used.
- `Game_PracticeProgramme_HandlePenaltyIssued`: L1 -> L3 only together with a verify row.
- A crash in 0x1429a0620..0x1429a0647 (EA 0x1429999a0..0x1429999c7) with the guard armed demotes the entry site and keeps the guard off.

## Build
- exe: F1_25.exe sha256 d873a1c8d2044cef2fd76c99c9d5a97649c5b815d055540995105f46619865ae (Steam). Repeat on the EA App build (pe_timestamp 1789128892, the function at 0x1429999a0) when that copy is available.
- loader and core: `native/dist/` of branch `fix/acclim-guard-1a8`, through a launcher run from that checkout (or `SWOFTY_LOADER_DIR=<checkout>/native/dist`); record the `swofty_core.dll` md5 from the build log.
- addons: an imported track (Hockenheim, Sochi or Delhi) placed in a Driver Career calendar.
- install: the launcher places `version.dll` and `swofty/`; nothing else.

## Trace config
None. The `[acclim]` and `[core]` lines in `loader.log` are the evidence.

## User steps
1. Launch through the launcher with the profile above. At the home screen, check `loader.log` for the guard lines (below).
2. Driver Career: continue or start a career whose next weekend is the imported track. Enter Practice 1 and leave the practice programme choice as it comes.
3. Leave the garage and cut a corner or run wide off track until the game shows a track limits warning; do it three times so a penalty follows. Drive one more full lap.
4. Control: Practice 1 at a stock track (Bahrain or Spain), pick Track Acclimatisation, run wide at a gate and check the gate shows as failed as before.
5. Quit from the main menu and keep `loader.log`.

## Expected log lines
- `core mixin set 'swofty_core\.dll \(track acclimatisation penalty guard\)': 1 mixin` → the guard was offered.
- `\[core\] site site_Game_AcclimPenalty_Entry at 0x[0-9a-f]+ \(base\+0x29a0620\) installed` (Steam) or `base\+0x29999a0` (EA) → the site resolved and the hook is in.
- `\[acclim\] track acclimatisation penalty guard armed` → the set installed.
- `\[acclim\] Track Acclimatisation programme 0x[0-9a-f]+ has no targets at this track \(targets 0x0\); penalty flag skipped` at the imported track after the first warning → the guard met the programme that faulted. No such line at the stock track in step 4.
Pass rule: steps 2 to 4 finish without a crash, the first three lines are logged, the skip line appears at the imported track, and the gates fail as before at the stock track. Fail rule: any crash with `F1_25.exe+0x29a06` or `+0x29999` (EA) in its stack, or the guard lines missing.
