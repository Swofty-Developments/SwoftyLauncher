# T337 R&D colour table written through game_mem::write and put back by restore

- Requested by: vmknolle (sdk-data-write), 2026-11-21
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
Does `swofty::game_mem::write` (core export `SwoftyCoreGameMem`) change `k_Career_RnDDeptColourTable` [4] and [5] in game, so the R&D overview paints the two extra departments in their colours, and does `restore` put the stock white back without a crash?

## Hypothesis
The table is `uint32_t[6]` in a read-only, non-code section of the Steam exe (`.sdata` on PE timestamp 1794312636, 0x145757fa8). The core makes the 4 bytes writable for the copy and puts the protection back; every reader of the table (16 riprel reads, `k_Career_RnDDeptColourTable` record) sees the new value on its next read. `restore` copies the saved 0xffffffff back.

## Promotes
- `SwoftyCoreGameMem` write_data / restore_data (core, experimental)
- `k_Career_RnDDeptColourTable` (globals): [4] and [5] are read for the box grid and the card colour byte (+0x948)

## Build
- exe: F1_25.exe Steam, PE timestamp 1794312636 (sha256 of the user's copy in the log)
- loader / core: this PR's `version.dll` and `swofty/addons/core/` from the CI artifact
- plugins: the season add-on's `rnd_departments.dll` built against `swofty/game_mem.hpp` (A1 switched from VirtualProtect to `game_mem::write(k_Career_RnDDeptColourTable, i, colour, 0xffffffffu)`, restore on its setting off)
- install: only `version.dll` and `swofty/`

## Trace config
None; loader.log lines only.

## User steps
1. Start the game, My Team career, open R&D overview: note the colours of the 5th and 6th department boxes.
2. Switch the add-on's extra-department setting off (or the add-on's restore option), reopen the R&D overview.
3. Quit to the main menu and back into the career.

## Expected log lines
- `\[rnd-dept\] colour table: \[0\]=ffeead00 .* \[4\]=ffffffff \[5\]=ffffffff` → stock before the write
- no `\[game-mem\] .*write_data\(k_Career_RnDDeptColourTable\)` refusal line → the core accepted both writes
Pass rule: step 1 shows the add-on's colours, step 2 white again, no crash. Fail rule: a `[game-mem]` refusal, stock white in step 1, or a crash (attach Send Logs).
