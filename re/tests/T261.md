# T261 Does a slowed crew rate or a deferred SendToTrack lengthen a pit stop safely (refuelling time)?

- Requested by: engine-re, 2026-11-18
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
Can a refuelling add-on add F seconds to a race pit stop by one of two static routes, without an early release, a stuck car or a crash, and how does the crew look?
- Route A: slower crew. The thunk `Game_PitLane_GetPitStopTimeRatio_Thunk` 0x1434d0cb0 returns out/(T+F) instead of N/T, and the pit lane record failsafe rec+0xe8 is raised by F.
- Route B: hold after the work. `GarageCrewMsg_SendToTrack` (msg 0 into `Game_PitLane_OnGarageCrewMessage` 0x1434cebe0) is deferred by F seconds while the car is in status 4, and rec+0xe8 is raised by F.

## Hypothesis
Static reading (game.md Inbox, E2 refuel round 2026-11-18):
- The stop ends at the crew's SendToTrack (status 4 -> 5) or at the failsafe timer rec+0xe4 > rec+0xe8 + penalty (`Game_PitLane_UpdateMovement` 0x1434ba0bb). rec = [[PitLaneSystem+0x48]+0x938+car*16].
- Route A slows the whole crew animation by T/(T+F) (one rate for all crew items). Without the rec+0xe8 raise, the failsafe releases the car before the crew finishes.
- Route B keeps the crew at normal speed; the car waits after the job-complete point. The JC event (frame 77 of the 21 family) comes after jack_move (frame 73), so the car would wait on the ground.
- Both routes only change time. Fuel still has to be written (FuelTank +0x100 via car+0x540, as Swofty core race_fuel.hpp does), because the stop has no fuel step.

Pass rule: t5 - t4 = stock stop + F (within 0.2 s), the car leaves normally and the tyres are changed. Fail rule: early release, the car stays in the box, or a crash.
