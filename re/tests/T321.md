# T321 More than 21 marshal_light_zone items with UDP telemetry on: corrupt session packet or crash?

- Requested by: engine-re, 2026-11-20

## Question
With 30 marshal_light_zone items on the main TrackSpace markup and UDP telemetry enabled, do the UDP session packet fields after the 21 marshal zones (safety car status and following) carry zone bytes, and does the game stay stable?

## Hypothesis
`Sess_CopyOptionsToState` 0x142cfaec0 writes the zone count as a byte to state+0xb3 (0x142cfb727) and copies EVERY zone as 5 bytes from state+0xb4 (loop 0x142cfb750..0x142cfb799); the cleared area is 21 zones (+0xb4..+0x11c). Zones 22+ overwrite state+0x11d onwards; +0x11d is rewritten later (0x142cfba89), the rest stays corrupted. Race logic (MarshalZoneMonitor, flags) has no cap.

## Promotes
- `Sess_CopyOptionsToState` marshal-zone note to L3; tracks.md#ai-and-track-markup-size-limits marshal row.

## Build
- exe: 1.0.145 runtime; swofty_core.
- data: own-track package with 30 marshal_light_zone items (IDs 1..30, ordered by Start); control package with 21.

## Trace config
1. Hook 0x142cfaec0 return: dump state+0xb0..+0x180.
2. Capture the UDP session packet (any F1 25 UDP listener) during the race.

## User steps
1. Settings: UDP telemetry on (2025 format). 2. Load the 21-zone package, race 2 laps, quit. 3. Same with the 30-zone package.

## Expected log lines
- numMarshalZones = 30 in the packet and non-zero garbage in m_safetyCarStatus / following fields -> proves the uncapped copy.
- no crash in both runs -> proves UDP-only data corruption.
Pass rule: 21 clean, 30 shows corrupted fields after zone 21, no crash. Fail rule: 30 is capped at 21 (then a cap exists elsewhere) or the game crashes.
