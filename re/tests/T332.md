# T332 Do the engineer radio, the wheel display or the UDP car status follow a fuel-mix change?

- Requested by: engine-re, 2026-11-20
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
When the player changes the fuel mix on the MFD, does the engineer say anything, does a Thrustmaster wheel display change, and does the UDP car-status fuel-mix byte follow at once?

## Hypothesis
No fuel-mix speech string was found in the exe (only 'low_fuel_mode', 'EngineLeanFuel'); speech is data-driven and was not read. `Game_ThrustmasterLcd_UpdateSectors` copies fuel_mode to its state +0x200 (0x142fd9ecb), reader not traced. The UDP car status takes the raw fuel_mode byte (+0x1954 of the car entry, 0x142d0066b in `Game_TelemetryUdp_CollectDrsZones`). For the virtual route all three would show the carrier mix.

## Promotes
- `Game_TelemetryUdp_CollectDrsZones` (fuel byte), notes on `Game_ThrustmasterLcd_UpdateSectors`.

## Build
- stock game plus UDP telemetry on (any listener tool); swofty_core trace-only.

## Trace config
1. Log CarFuelEcu+0x128 of the player when it changes.

## User steps
1. Grand Prix race, own car, UDP telemetry on. 2. Change the mix lean -> standard -> rich and back, 10 s apart. 3. Note any engineer radio line about the mix.

## Expected log lines
- UDP fuel mix equals the logged fuel_mode within one packet.
Pass rule: UDP follows; radio lines (if any) noted. Fail rule: UDP lags or shows another value.
