## Hypothesis
`Trk_GrandPrixTrackSelectVm_OnCommandRaised` (vt_Trk_GrandPrixTrackSelectVm slot 10, `tracks.grand_prix_track_select_vm_on_command_raised`) is the Grand Prix track select's command handler; advance_pressed 0xfc8f026c is its confirm and does nothing unless vm+0x93b is set (kb:subsystems/tracks.md#select). The mode byte is the game setup mode stat id (swofty_f1 is 20, f1_26 13); entrant_list is the box's selected list and season its u16 at +0xb0.
