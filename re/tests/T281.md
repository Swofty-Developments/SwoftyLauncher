# T281 Does a per-car VTF BrakeATCStrength floor give traction control with the TC assist Off?

- Requested by: engine-re, 2026-11-18
- Status: draft
- Session: (filled by the lead when batched)

## Question
With TC Off in the menu, does a car whose VTF `BrakeECU/BrakeATCStrength` is 1.0 (or 0.5) cut throttle on wheelspin, for the player car and for an AI car, and what solver +0x504 / BrakeECU +0x21e0 values does the trace show?

## Hypothesis
`Car_Solver_PrepareDrivetrain` 0x1446db280 stores solver +0x504 = max(CarObfFloat BrakeECU +0x2098 (BrakeATCStrength), BrakeECU +0x21e0) at 0x1446db793. `Car_Powertrain_Step` evaluates the ATC splines at +0x504 for a non-AI car (0x1446e2482, 0x1446e24c4) and uses +0x504 directly as the throttle factor for an AI car (CarLive+0x124 == 1). So the VTF value is a per-car minimum TC level that the menu cannot lower. The stock 2025 value was not read here (lead: read it from the shipped VTF).
